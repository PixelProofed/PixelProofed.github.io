Monster Guide

Monsters are the various creatures that inhabit this world, acting as the main threat to all Humans and Demi-humans. They differ from the regular animals that inhabit the world in that, like humans, they have access to the System, including Stats, Traits, and Skills. Though many monsters are able to populate through normal breeding, the world often creates them directly, with monsters spawning into existence in places where natural ambient mana has gathered. While most monsters only they one trait, they are all capable of growing stronger than the natives of this world, with a max stat total of 800. What makes them truly dangerous, however, is their potential to evolve. If a monster meets the conditions to evolve, they will be able to reach a total of 4 traits throughout their growth, one for each stage. Luckily for the natives, most monsters cannot evolve, due to the strict conditions. Monsters will only ever be able to evolve if they were born at an Origin stage and formed a Mana Stone before reaching level 15. If a monster meets these conditions and evolve at level 15, they will be able to continue doing so. However, due to the savage nature of monsters even against each other, and the formation of the Adventurer's Guild, most do not live long enough to do so. This is why Third Evolution monsters are all extremely rare. When monsters of a higher species breed, their children can be either of the same species, or of any lower species along their line, including the Origin, assuming their are species that can be born in their line. In there are no species that can be born, that monster is incapable of breeding. Sometimes monsters are born with skills they wouldn't have been able to learn otherwise. For example, an Imp wouldn't have been able to learn Fire Ball, due to not actually having a Fire Affinity, but they may be born with it. Due to being connected to the System, all monsters are influenced by the god-given language granted by Siras. Because of this, all monsters with the Sapient tag are capable understanding and speaking the language. This does not mean they all will-- many monsters have no interest in verbal conversations.

In this setting, most of an Adventurer's coin is going to come from slaying and harvesting monsters for materials and Mana Stones. Monster parts are used in all different kinds of professions, with greater value put on those of higher rarity, so it's an easy sell, which the Adventurer's Guild always willing to buy as the indisputable go-between. Mana stones, however, are the big ticket items. While most monsters won't have these, there's always a chance to find one while harvesting, and as you get to taking down stronger monsters, the rank and value of these stones jump dramatically. Mana stones are largely used by mages, in alchemy, and by the artificers who use them to make Mana Stone Devices, such as the street lamps seen throughout the city. As the lowest ranks of these stones are disposable, they are in extremely high demand at all times. There is also the chance of gathering simple pieces of gear, or even potions, from the more humanoid monsters.

One of the character types is Reincarnated Monster. Like normal Reincarnates, these are monsters with memories of their past life. How much they remember is up to the player. This allows one to play as a monster with the mind of a human, rather than just savage instinct. All Reincarnated Monsters have to have been human in their past life, from a world with no magic or monsters of its own, ensuring the experience is new and scary. They will need to have been 18+ before their death. Each will start as an Origin stage monster from one of the Evolution Trees, starting at lvl 1 with a 50 stat points to distribute freely. All Reincarnated Monsters have a Mana Stone in them by default, meaning they are all able to evolve. All of the Evolution Trees can be found by clicking below, or in the sidebar. Evolution Trees

Monsters that have met the requirements will be able to evolve up to three times throughout their growth. The first time will be upon reaching level 15, or 153 stat points. The second time will be upon reaching level 40, or 342 stat points. The last time will be at level 80, or 645 stat points. Upon evolving, monsters gain certain growth benefits. First, they get an additional trait from their new species. Then, they gain bonus stat points depending on the stage. It will be 10 points for first evolution, 15 points for second evolution, and 25 points for third evolution. Lastly, they will gain an Evo Skill. This is an automatically gained skill that can have a bit of spunk to it. Like Born Skills, even if the monster doesn't have an affinity trait, if there's certain affinity concepts that fit their species, their evolution skill may be magic based. This is how demons and such might end up with Fire Magic skills. When your monster character is evolving, you will submit an updated sheet with their current stats, new form, and Evo Skill to Character Submissions, with the Evolving tag. The character sheet of each stage will be kept in their Accepted section.
