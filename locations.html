Woodcress

Inhabitants
Humans, Beastkin, Genasi
Terrain
---

Description

Woodcress is the main city of the RP region and the center of Balethorn. It sits where two major rivers meet, with low hills shaping its districts and farmland spreading outward beyond its walls and roads. It is the largest concentration of trade, law, religion, and organized adventuring in the region, and most official business between territories eventually passes through it.

Life Here

The city is built for traffic, production, and control. Merchant streets, workshops, markets, civic buildings, temples, and noble estates all have their own place, with the Commons and trade districts doing the daily work that keeps the city running. Human architecture dominates, but Beastkin and Genasi influences are visible in courtyards, elemental features, and mixed-use buildings that reflect its diverse working population.

The Plains

Inhabitants
Humans, Beastkin, Genasi, Centaurs
Terrain
Grasslands, rivers, groves, open fields

Description

The Plains are the agricultural backbone of Balethorn, made up of broad grasslands, winding waterways, scattered tree groves, and long stretches of workable land. The terrain is open enough for easy movement, large herds, and cavalry travel, but broad enough that distance and exposure still matter. Most food, livestock, and day-to-day rural life in the region comes out of this territory.

Life Here

Human villages, Beastkin homesteads, Genasi-adapted farming settlements, and roaming Centaur camps all have a place here. The land favors communities that can farm, herd, or move with the season, and many roads across the region are really just repeated trade routes and migration paths. Outside the cultivated parts, the Barrens, ruins, and monster territory remind people how quickly the land stops being gentle.

The Shores

Inhabitants
Merfolk, Lamia, scattered Humans
Terrain
Beaches, cliffs, coves, reefs, estuaries

Description

The Shores are a coastal territory of beaches, rocky cliffs, hidden coves, reefs, dunes, and tidal ground that changes with the sea. Life here is split between the land and the water rather than being fully one or the other. Merfolk hold the deeper coastal waters and reef settlements, while Lamia communities move and gather along the shore itself in places shaped by tide, fishing, and ritual.

Life Here

This region is built around access to the sea. Merfolk cities and palaces exist below the waterline, while Lamia tribes keep more temporary or semi-nomadic settlements along beaches, coves, and sheltered coast. Neutral places like the Lagoon and Moonshell Beach matter because the coastline is shared by many tribes and routines tied to the moon, the tide, and the safety of molting or mating seasons.

The Mountains

Inhabitants
Centaurs, Kobolds, Humans
Terrain
Peaks, slopes, plateaus, caves, springs

Description

The Mountains are steep, cold, and difficult country, with forested lower slopes giving way to stone, snow, cliffs, narrow passes, and deep cave systems. Travel is slower, resources are harder won, and the terrain itself decides what can be built and where. Ore, stone, hot springs, and isolated valleys make the territory valuable enough that people continue to live and work there despite the risks.

Life Here

Centaurs hold sturdy lodges on usable plateaus, Kobolds expand cave networks into defended warrens, and Humans establish practical mining settlements where extraction is worth the danger. Much of the territory rewards resilience and preparation more than size or comfort, and even lawful communities exist close to caves, monster dens, outlaw routes, and rough ground that can become deadly very quickly.

The Vale

Inhabitants
Vampires, Tengu, Changelings, Humans
Terrain
Forests, fog, marshes, valleys

Description

The Vale is a dim, secluded territory trapped in long shadow between mountain ranges. Dense old forest, lingering fog, marsh ground, caves, and low light define the land, giving it a reputation for secrecy and unease even before one factors in who lives there. The terrain favors hidden roads, controlled movement, and communities that would rather not be found quickly.

Life Here

Vampire centers are built around fortified estates and controlled settlements, Tengu keep elevated roosts and training sites, and Changelings use buried ruins and hidden passage networks to stay out of direct view. Human villages exist as well, but in much smaller, quieter form than in Balethorn. The whole territory feels shaped by concealment, selective trust, and old power that prefers to remain half-seen.

The Glades

Inhabitants
Elves, Fairies, Nymphs
Terrain
Forest, streams, meadows, lakes, clearings

Description

The Glades are an ancient forest territory of dense canopy, thick undergrowth, streams, lakes, meadows, and old clearings where nature and magic feel closely tied. Light is filtered, paths are not always obvious, and many of the region's most important places are hidden inside living woodland rather than built out into the open. It is one of the strongest centers of natural magic and rare plant life in the region.

Life Here

Elves build with living trees, Fairies gather in hidden glens and improvised natural structures, and Nymphs often live more as part of a place than inside a formal settlement at all. The territory supports communities built around preservation, herbal knowledge, sacred ground, and careful use of resources, with ruins, old groves, and natural dungeons reminding visitors that the forest is older than any current border.
