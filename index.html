Welcome To

NEW GAME PLUS

In this RP, characters live in part of a young fantasy world shaped by a litRPG System of stats, traits, skills, levels, and dungeons. Some are Natives who have known this world all their lives. Others are Reincarnators, born here with varying memory of another life. Others, still, are Transmigrators, suddenly pulled from their own worlds and dropped into this unfamiliar land by Siras, a god of creation, each grants a random couple of traits and a skill in the process.

The story is centered on life in a remote and generally dangerous corner of the continent. The city of Woodcress, the Plains, and the surrounding territories of The Mountains, The Shores, The Vale, and The Glades. Here, people make names for themselves largely through adventurer work such as monster hunting and dungeon delving, but one can also get by on trade, crafting, occasionally faith, and the relationships they build across the region of the RP. Whether as a hero, a wanderer, a local, a monster, or something in between.
